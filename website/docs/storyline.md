# Satyam Xavier's — Website Storyline

**Status:** draft for review · 2026-10-03
**Site:** `sxebschool.com` · English and Nepali
**Rule for every fact on the site:** verified with the school, or marked **TBC** until it is.

---

## 1. Who visits, and what each needs

| Visitor | They arrive asking | They leave having |
|---|---|---|
| **A family choosing a school** (the main audience) | "Is this a good, safe place for my child? What is it like? How do we join?" | Felt what a day there is like, seen proof, and called or started admission |
| **A current parent** | "Where do I sign in? What's on this week?" | Reached the parent app in one tap, seen the latest notices |
| **A student** | "What's happening at school?" | Seen events, results, their school on display |
| **A teacher applying** | "Is this a good place to work?" | Seen the school's values and how to apply |
| **Alumni and the community** | "What has become of my school?" | Seen its history and where it is going |

Most visitors arrive **on a phone, on mobile data**, often from a link shared in a family group. The first screen has to work in a few seconds and say who we are without scrolling.

---

## 2. The one idea: *growing up here*

A child can walk through this school's gate at three and leave at sixteen, after their SEE. The whole site tells that journey: **Nursery to Class 10, and on into the world.**

- On the home page, **scrolling is growing up**: one continuous line (the "path") runs down the page and the child grows with it, through each stage of school.
- Every section answers one question a parent has at that moment of the scroll, in the order they would ask it.
- The tone is warm and confident: a school speaking to families in Hetauda, not a brochure.

Working tagline (TBC with the school): **"From first steps to SEE, and beyond."**

## 4. Home page, scene by scene

Each scene: what it says, what it shows, how it moves, and what we need from the school. Motion has a job in every scene; nothing moves just to move. Everything has a calm, still version for visitors who switch animations off.

### Scene 0 — The gate (first screen)
- **Says:** Satyam Xavier's English Boarding School · Hetauda · Nursery to Class 10 · since 2059 BS.
- **Shows:** the school's own photos in an arch (the gate): the little ones with balloons in front of the building, slowly crossfading to the building and the Class 10 batch. Photos only, no video: video costs storage and data on a phone.
- **Moves:** the name writes itself in, letter by letter; the photo rises into the arch with a slow zoom. A small "scroll" cue grows the start of the path.
- **Actions:** Start admission · Call · Parent login (top right, always).
- **Need:** 3 strong photos (have them, from the school's Facebook page).

### Scene 1 — Our promise
- **Says:** the school's motto and one sentence on what it promises a child. **TBC:** the real motto.
- **Moves:** words rise into place one line at a time; the path continues.
- **Need:** the motto, in English and Nepali.

### Scene 2 — The journey begins: Early years (Nursery, LKG, UKG)
- **Says:** how small children are cared for: play, first letters, safety, warm teachers.
- **Shows:** bright photos of the little ones.
- **Moves:** the path becomes a small child's footsteps; photos settle in like cards being laid on a table.
- **Need:** 4–6 early-years photos; a line on class size or care. **TBC**

### Scene 3 — Growing: Primary and lower secondary (Class 1–8)
- **Says:** strong foundations in English, Nepali, maths and science; the habits of learning.
- **Shows:** the eight classes as a **horizontal strip** you swipe or scroll through, Class 1 to Class 8, each with a photo and one line.
- **Moves:** the page pins and scrolls sideways through the years; the child on the path grows taller.
- **Need:** a photo and one line per stage or class.

### Scene 4 — The summit: Class 9–10 and SEE
- **Says:** preparation for the SEE, and what results our students achieve. **TBC:** real results (pass rate, top grades).
- **Moves:** numbers count up as they come into view; the path climbs.
- **Need:** SEE results for recent years, with permission to publish.

### Scene 5 — Life beyond the classroom
- **Says:** sports, music, dance, art, clubs, trips, celebrations (Dashain, Tihar, Sports Day, Parents' Day…). **TBC** which ones.
- **Shows:** a lively photo mosaic.
- **Moves:** tiles drift at slightly different speeds as you scroll (gentle depth); tapping one opens it full screen.
- **Need:** 15–30 event photos.

### Scene 6 — The school in numbers
- **Says:** years since founding (from 2059 BS), classes, teachers, students, SEE pass rate. **Only real numbers. TBC** all except founding year and classes.
- **Moves:** each number counts up once, when it comes into view.

### Scene 7 — A word from the principal
- **Says:** a short, warm message to families.
- **Shows:** the principal's photo and signature.
- **Moves:** the photo comes into focus; the message appears as if written.
- **Need:** the principal's name (**TBC** — a public listing says Badri Pudasaini), photo, and a 100–150 word message.

### Scene 8 — Families trust us
- **Says:** two or three short quotes from parents and former students.
- **Moves:** one quote at a time, changing slowly; never auto-advances while being read.
- **Need:** real quotes with permission (names or initials).

### Scene 9 — Join us: admissions
- **Says:** admissions are open for the coming year (**TBC** dates); three simple steps: visit or call → bring documents → admission.
- **Moves:** the path arrives at an open gate; the three steps light up in turn.
- **Actions:** Call the school · Get directions · Enquiry form.

### Scene 10 — Already part of the family?
- **Says:** "Parents and staff: sign in to the school app" — attendance, marks, fees, notices.
- **Shows:** a phone showing the parent home screen of our app.
- **Moves:** the phone slides up with a real screen.

### Scene 11 — Find us (footer)
- **Shows:** map of Hetauda-4 (**TBC** exact pin), phone, email, office hours, social links, both languages.
- **The path ends** at the school's pin on the map: the journey loops back to the gate.

## 6. Voice and look

- **Voice:** warm, simple, confident; short sentences; every English line has a Nepali one, written by a Nepali speaker rather than translated word for word.
- **Look:** the school's logo blue as the main colour (the same family as the app, so the site and the app feel like one school); generous white space; real photos large.
- **Motion:** smooth scrolling; scroll-led reveals; one pinned horizontal section; counting numbers; never motion that blocks reading. A calm version for anyone who turns animations off. It must stay smooth on a mid-range phone.
- **Speed:** the first screen readable in under 3 seconds on mobile data. Photos only, no video.
- **Two full languages:** every page exists in English (`/en/…`, the default) and Nepali (`/ne/…`), switched from "EN · नेपाली" in the nav; the choice is remembered. Each headline keeps one line in the other language. Nepali text uses Mukta with taller line spacing and no negative letter-spacing; years and counts in Nepali digits (२०५९), phone numbers in Western digits. Every Nepali line is read by a Nepali speaker at the school before it goes live.
- **Dark mode:** automatic, following the phone's setting; no extra button. Deep navy ground (#0B1630), raised navy cards, light text; the building's yellow and the crest crimson stay as accents. Same family as the app's dark theme.

## 7. Creative laws

Adapted from the planning method of the /brag skill ([latent-spaces/brag](https://github.com/latent-spaces/brag)), which plans launch films the same way we need to plan this story. These hold for every scene and page.

1. **The hook is everything.** The first screen decides whether a parent keeps scrolling. Plan Scene 0 before anything else: one strong moving image and the school's name, nothing competing with it.
2. **Show the real school.** Every scene shows something real from this school: its children, its building, its events, its own words. No stock photos, no abstract filler. If a real photo is not ready, the scene waits; it does not get a stand-in.
3. **Specific, never generic.** It must feel made for this school in Hetauda, not any school. Banned unless the school proves it with something specific: "nurturing tomorrow's leaders", "holistic development", "world-class education", "state-of-the-art facilities", "a home away from home".
4. **Readable motion.** Motion keeps the pace; text never flashes. A short label stays settled for at least 0.8 s, a sentence about 0.3 s per word, before anything moves it on. Fast in, then hold; never fast in, then gone.
5. **One idea per scene.** No bullet lists in the story. A scene says one thing and shows it.
6. **Honest claims.** Every number, result and promise is the school's own and verified with them (section 8). Nothing the school cannot stand behind on a parent's phone in front of them.
7. **The shape.** Hook → who we are → the journey (two or three moments that land) → proof → the way in (admission, call, visit).

### Tone

**Cinematic and alive (changed 2026-10-03).** The first direction ("restraint over spectacle") produced a site the user called "too basic, like a 2020 school website". Families compare it with the AI-made sites they see in reels, so the site must feel made in 2026:
- Dark cinematic ground; giant display type mixed with an italic serif; grain; frosted glass.
- Motion carries the story: a loader that counts Nursery → SEE, balloons that float and dodge the cursor, a zoom through the arch-shaped gate, a pinned horizontal "growing up" section with a filling height-chart ruler, a "Now in Class 4" pill that tracks the scroll, velocity marquees, outline numbers that fill as they count, 3D-tilt photo tiles, magnetic buttons, a giant footer wordmark.
- Every effect is tied to this school (balloons from the hero photo, the gate, the ruler, the classes). No generic effects.
- Still fast and kind: lighter on phones, a calm version for reduced motion, photos only.
Live prototype: `Workspace/sxebs-website-prototype/index.html` (GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis).

### Share preview

Most visits start from a link shared in a family group (Viber, WhatsApp, Messenger), so the preview is part of the story:
- **Preview image:** the best single frame of Scene 0, picked on purpose (the gate in the morning, the name readable), not whatever the first frame happens to be.
- **Preview text:** one sentence a parent would forward, in English and Nepali. Draft (TBC): *"Satyam Xavier's, Hetauda — from first steps to SEE. Admissions open."*
- The same for every main page (About, Admissions, an event), so each shared link looks deliberate.
