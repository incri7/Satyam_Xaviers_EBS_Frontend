# 3D scroll references

Top-tier Three.js sites to study for the v2 "building climb" home page. Each one is Awwwards or FWA recognised. Awards and techniques come from the linked sources (checked 2026-10-03); open each site on a phone as well as a desktop before borrowing from it.

| # | Site | Award | What makes it impressive | Technique | Lesson for our site |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Igloo Inc** by abeto · [igloo.inc](https://igloo.inc) · [case study](https://www.awwwards.com/igloo-inc-case-study.html) | Awwwards Site of the Year and Developer Site of the Year 2024; FWA of the Day | Scroll drifts the camera through a frozen landscape; each project sits inside its own ice block | Three.js, three-mesh-bvh, Svelte, GSAP; ice blocks grown procedurally by a crystal-growth algorithm; chromatic aberration and frost dissolves between scenes | One continuous camera move, with a transition effect between stages, reads as a story rather than a page |
| 2 | **Oryzo** by Lusion · [oryzo.ai](https://oryzo.ai) · [behind the scenes](https://blog.lusion.co/oryzo-bts-part-3-7-website-ux-ui-and-illustrations) | Awwwards Site of the Month (April 2026) and Developer Award | A single hero object, lit and animated with real weight; a quiet interface lets it carry the page | Three.js; easing that mimics inertia; scroll moves the camera through real Z depth instead of sliding 2D layers | Sell one object properly. Our object is the building: one model, well lit, beats a busy scene |
| 3 | **Cartier Watches & Wonders** by Immersive Garden · [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/cartier-watches-wonders-2025) · [studio page](https://immersive-g.com/projects/cartier-watches-and-wonders-23/) | Awwwards Site of the Day (several editions); studio named Awwwards Agency of the Year 2025 | A digital twin of the pavilion; you scroll through six alcoves like rooms in a museum | Three.js for WebGL, Blender for assets, GSAP and Lenis for motion and scroll, Web Audio for sound | Closest to our idea: a real building, entered space by space. Each floor of the school can be one "room" of the story |
| 4 | **Hubtown** by Unseen Studio · [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/hubtown) | Awwwards Site of the Day (June 2026) and Developer Award | A real-estate developer's 40-year portfolio framed as one glowing 3D monolith over a reflective landscape | Three.js hero scene with GSAP; a mouse-reveal interaction where the cursor uncovers detail in geometry and lighting | A building company made a building the hero. Shows the "architect's model" look can carry an institution's brand |
| 5 | **Explore Primland** · [explore.ownprimland.com](https://explore.ownprimland.com) · [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/explore-primland) | Awwwards Site of the Day (February 2026) | A cinematic aerial flythrough makes a remote place explorable from anywhere | Real terrain rendered in Three.js with atmospheric fog; camera glides over it on scroll; GSAP; Blender | For a physical place, a scroll-driven flythrough conveys scale and setting that photos cannot. Useful for the approach to the gate |
| 6 | **Sébastien Lempens** portfolio · [sebastien-lempens.com](https://sebastien-lempens.com) · [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/sebastien-lempens) · [Codrops write-up](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/04/28/more-than-a-portfolio-building-a-scroll-driven-3d-world-with-something-to-say/) | Awwwards Site of the Day; also FWA and CSS Design Awards | A scroll-driven tour through a 3D Paris, switching between first-person, following a scooter, and skydiving | React, Three.js, Blender; camera path driven by scroll | Built on React + Three.js, the same family as our stack. Shows how to change camera mood between story chapters |
| 7 | **Jordan Breton** portfolio · [jordan-breton.com](https://jordan-breton.com) · [FWA case](https://thefwa.com/cases/a-3d-game-like-portfolio) | FWA Site of the Day (2 October 2025) | A floating island full of small living details: grass, waterfall, fire, wind, trees, butterflies | Three.js; camera moves between fixed viewpoints; many small animated elements | Small ambient life (a flag moving, light changing) makes a static model feel alive. Our flag and balconies are candidates |
| 8 | **Bruno Simon** portfolio · [bruno-simon.com](https://bruno-simon.com) · [case study](https://www.awwwards.com/brunos-portfolio-case-study.html) | Awwwards Site of the Month (November; again January 2026) | You drive a toy car around a miniature 3D world to find the content | Three.js with a physics engine; destructible objects; bird's-eye camera | The benchmark for playful 3D. Too game-like for our main path, but a good model for an optional "explore the school" mode |
| 9 | **Lusion** studio site · [lusion.co](https://lusion.co) · [case study](https://www.awwwards.com/case-study-for-lusion-by-lusion-winner-of-site-of-the-month-may.html) | Awwwards Site of the Month (May) | An abstract 3D scene that reacts to the mouse and morphs as you scroll | Three.js; vertex animation compressed (11 keyframes for 66 frames, 16-bit data), with separate desktop and mobile versions of the assets | Ship a lighter asset set for phones. Most of our visitors arrive on mobile data |
| 10 | **Messenger** by abeto · [Korben write-up](https://korben.info/en/abeto-messenger-mail-carrier-mini-planet.html) · [Hacker News thread](https://brianlovin.com/hn/45396441) | Awwwards Site of the Day | A multiplayer game on a tiny planet, in the browser, with no install | Three.js, three-mesh-bvh, models from Houdini and Blender; loads at 5.7 MB | Proof that a rich 3D world can load in a few MB. Our target budget (`school.glb` is 1.4 MB) is realistic |

## Common stack across these sites

Three.js (or React Three Fiber on top of it), GSAP with ScrollTrigger to map scroll to the camera, Lenis for smooth scroll, and Blender for the models. This matches the stack planned in `website/README.md` (Vite + React Three Fiber + GSAP).
