## 2. The real building, compared with our 3D model

**Layout of the site:**
- From the street, a corner of yellow compound wall carries the blue signboard: crest, English and Devanagari name, "Hetauda-4, Chisapani", and both phones (IMG_3014).
- A **long dirt driveway** leads in from the street. It is **40–60 m**, by eye from the photos.
- **Three yellow school buses** (Eicher and Tata Marcopolo) park at its end, in front of the building.
- A white neighbouring building with brown roller shutters stands on the left of the driveway; the video's hand points at it.

**The main block** (IMG_3017, 3082, 3084, 3086):
- **Shape:** a long, narrow concrete slab building. Its **short end faces the driveway**, and the long side faces a side yard.
- **Height:** **four classroom floors** with open corridors. The ground floor is raised over a **semi-basement**, reached down a **ramp** to a collapsible steel gate. The ramp is under the front corridor (IMG_3020).
- **Roof:** flat, with **rebar left up for a future floor**, a small stair head, an AC unit and the **Nepal flag**.
- **Corridors:** cantilevered balconies on every floor, **along the long side and wrapping round the front end**, with **rounded corners**.
  - **Slab edges:** painted **slate blue**, streaked by rain, with blue brackets under each slab.
  - **Supports:** **thin stainless-steel posts** from slab to slab.
  - **Railings:** **stainless steel with vertical bars**.
- **Walls:** two-tone, **pale yellow below** and **white above** the door heads. Doors and windows have **dark brown frames** and grilles.
- **Inside:**
  - **Stairs:** at the ends, with granite steps, black nosing stripes and steel railings.
  - **Fittings:** collapsible gates on openings, granite wash basins on every floor, tiled backsplashes (sea and floral), toilets.

**Next to it:**
- **An older yellow house:** 3 storeys, joined to the block. It has **terracotta Newari-style bands**, an arched window, a small gabled porch, and fluted columns with golden capitals. **Its use is unknown.**
- **The yard:** a bare dirt yard, banana trees, and concrete-block walls.
- **Behind:** a **blue-roofed single-storey shed**, and a **playground on blue rubber matting** with a green tube slide and a red spiral slide.
- **A side alley:** narrow, along a brick wall.
- **The view:** the Mahabharat hills behind Hetauda, seen from the top corridors.

**Inside the classrooms** (IMG_3061–3074):
- **Structure and walls:** **red ceiling beams and columns**, white walls over a **dark grey dado**.
- **Painted teaching murals:**
  - the Devanagari alphabet;
  - multiplication tables;
  - the months;
  - animals;
  - shapes;
  - festivals;
  - **"National symbols of Nepal"**: flag, coat of arms, Danphe, rhododendron.
- **Furniture and equipment:**
  - **benches with red cushioned seats** and long wooden desks;
  - a **whiteboard** (not a blackboard);
  - ceiling fans, a projector and a speaker;
  - a red cupboard;
  - a teacher's chair.
- **Door:** a "Welcome to our class" sign.
- **Other:** a friendly white dog appears in several shots.

**Offices** (IMG_3088–3098):
- the principal's office, with a desk and nameplate;
- the accounts office, behind a steel-bar counter;
- a carpeted staff room with a projector and whiteboard.

**What our current model gets wrong** (the "realistic" brief):
1. **Placement:** the building stands deep in the plot at the end of a driveway, with buses in front. It is not a wide front facing a gate on a forecourt.
2. **Orientation:** the **short end faces the arrival**, and the long balconied side faces the side yard.
3. **Base and roof:** there is a semi-basement with a ramp, and a roof with rebar and a stair head. The model has neither, and it invents a separate stair tower.
4. **Missing neighbours:** the joined traditional house, the playground and the blue shed.
5. **Classrooms:** they have whiteboards, red beams and red-cushion benches, and murals. Our story's **chalk blackboard** is not what the school uses.
6. **What is right:** the colours (yellow and white walls, blue slab edges, rounded corners, steel railings).

## 3. Questions for the client before modelling

1. **Dimensions:** the length and width of the main block, or the number of classrooms per floor. Google Maps satellite at the coordinates above would give the footprint.
2. **Floors:** which classes are on which floor, and what is in the semi-basement.
3. **The neighbours:** what is the joined yellow house (hostel, office, owner's home)? And is the white shuttered building on the left of the driveway part of the school?
4. **Layout:** where the playground and the blue shed sit relative to the building, and which way north is.
5. **PG:** is there a play group level (the banner says "PG देखि")? It would sit before Nursery.
6. **Gate time:** does the gate really open at 6:00 AM? School starts at 9:25.
7. **Email:** which one is public, `admin@sxebschool.edu.np` (not yet set up) or `mailme.satyamxaviers@gmail.com`?
8. **The video:** it has narration we have not transcribed. Did it explain anything?
9. **Consent:** may we publish the photos that show a staff member (IMG_3020)?
